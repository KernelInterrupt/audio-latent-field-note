LATENT AUDIO FIELD NOTE

The codec floor is the result

In this transfer probe, target edits often sit at or below the ACE-Step round-trip floor. A single shared latent direction is measurable, but not yet a usable control.

1 · Start with the measured boundary

The main metric is the VAE training spectral loss. The floor is the encode→decode cost of the source; target distance is the requested stem edit; endpoint gain compares a latent edit with a latent no-op.

The frozen model is ACE-Step 1.5 Oobleck VAE: 48 kHz, 64 latent dimensions, and 2048× compression. MUSDB18-HQ is native 44.1 kHz; the evaluated windows are resampled to 48 kHz. Results below pool 100 four-second windows from 51 tracks.

3 · The shared direction is still too weak

The tested edits in this transfer probe do not support a usable shared global control direction. This is a scoped negative result, not an absolute claim about every possible global vector.

4 · Compression tracks difficulty, with limits

FLAC-5 and WavPack rank the 100 windows highly, but not identically: Spearman ρ = 0.996. In pooled held-out prediction over 50 windows, FLAC bits/sample correlates with endpoint error at ρ = 0.795 for ACE-Step, 0.769 for EnCodec 32 kHz, and 0.752 for Music2Latent. Content-only prediction has R² = 0.526; adding codec identity gives R² = 0.581, a gain of 5.5 percentage points.

The correlations and R² are pooled held-out results, not general laws.

5 · One compact rerun

The frozen inputs and read-only probe script are included alongside this page. Run the commands locally from the publish folder to reproduce the displayed projections: spine_results.json, why5_analysis.json, and probe.py (see README.md for the static-site note).

python3 probe.py floor
python3 probe.py oracle
python3 probe.py strata
